# RFC: The DnB identity graph — the honest identity surface, measured before it is built

**Status:** Final (research → /taste → 4-role adversarial panel synthesized, 2026-07-29) — completeness standard applied.
**For:** the operator first (§ Decisions is the deliverable), then a build session for whichever artifact is ruled in.
**Canon/authority:** the codebase, `LORE.md` / `PRODUCT.md` / `DESIGN.md` / `VOICE.md`, `docs/album-entity.md`, `docs/catalogue-crawler.md`, `docs/naming-conventions.md`. Planning, not spec. Companion evidence: [docs/planning/identity-graph-research.md](./planning/identity-graph-research.md) (the measured numbers, queries, and ToS clauses — committed so the evidence outlives the session that gathered it).

> Process note: four research threads (external landscape + two sub-verifications, internal substrate, ISRC correctness + platform ToS, monetization mechanics + persona), a /taste pass, and a four-role adversarial panel (staff engineer, product/scope, canon/brand, data-contract/legal). The panel's corrections are baked in — including three that inverted the draft: measurement now precedes commitment, the naming was wrong, and two contract states were uncomputable or canon-violating. Verifications in the companion file.

## The standard (definition of done)

Whatever artifact § Decisions rules in ships complete — tests, docs, copy gates, registry fan-out, nothing optional. The sanctioned "not now"s, stated as honest scoping: the Apple leg is blocked on an unread contract clause (external dependency — ADPLA §3.3.6(D) must be read verbatim before Apple links are served to third parties); demand and citation uptake are outcomes outside our control; and **the artifact choice itself is deliberately sequenced after Unit 0's measurement** — committing before measuring was the draft's inversion and the panel's chief correction. Dangling threads tied off regardless of artifact: the ISRC attempt stamp with its backfill semantics, the Discogs catalogue-row bookkeeping, and the evidence file.

## 0. Summary — the reframe, post-panel

- **The resolver is a READ of a ledger Fluncle already keeps — and the ledger is honest only where it actually records.** The anchor waterfall computes verification rung, attempts, caps, and refusals, and exposes none of it. But the panel's per-platform walk shows the honesty is _uneven_: fully computable for Spotify's uncertified rows, structurally never-terminal for Apple, one-shot for MusicBrainz, and **not computable at all for Deezer** (every failure path returns an indistinguishable `{}`). The public contract may only promise what a column can back, platform by platform.
- **The product is the honest negative — as a persona constraint, not a marketing line.** `absent` with real attempt state ("looked N times over three months; stop asking") is the claim no incumbent can retrofit, and it is a _selector's_ claim: it exists because a human gate exists. The resolver is never a data product and must never read like a database speaking; coverage is stated plainly, never advertised. This constrains the copy, not just the positioning.
- **Measure first, then choose the artifact.** Unit 0 (six read-only queries against hosted prod, thresholds pre-stated below) runs before the operator rules on anything. Then the choice is a ladder, cheapest first: extend the existing ops · a published identity dump · a `/resolve` reader page · a new keyed endpoint — each costed in § Alternatives, with the dump and the reader page noted as the two that feed the September label-outreach story directly.
- **Free is the recommendation, for the right reasons.** Not because free cures the Spotify-contract question (it does not — "standalone service" is a structural clause, not a price test) but because demand measures near zero (Linktree killed the canonical resolver rather than charge; ~80 dependent repos; indie WTP ≈ 0), the tax overhead of charging does not scale down, and the persona survives a gift better than a product. Posture B (money buys bulk/convenience, never access) remains the only credible later step; posture C is evidenced against.
- **The sunset is a tailwind, never a deadline.** Odesli's API returns `410 Gone` from 2026-07-31 and its free pages have already lost the big-three bridge in every direction (measured 6/6). The vacuum is permanent — the only successor is paid — so nothing here needs to race, and the freeze ruling is made calmly.

## 1. Context, goals, and what this surface is in persona terms

**Why now:** the roadmap called this "the highest-potential business seed"; the research demoted the business case and promoted the asset case — reach, the label-outreach receipts, and the keys/metering substrate three other parked items already need. **Honest calibration:** in reach — the trues-ups, the measurement, one chosen artifact, complete. Outside our control — integration uptake (the measurable ecosystem is tiny), citation, any revenue (no credible conversion figure exists anywhere; this RFC declines to invent one).

**In persona terms:** the surface is one thing only — _the honest negative a human gate makes possible._ No surface built here may compare Fluncle's coverage to anyone's, present the archive as a completeness claim, or carry a line that reads as a database speaking rather than an archive with a person behind it. The tier is carried by the `certified` boolean and by nothing else — no field name, enum value, reason string, heading, or description may contain a noun for the uncertified tier (the `MixTrackSchema` constraint, inherited verbatim).

## 2. Unit 0 — measure, with the thresholds stated before the numbers exist

Read-only, freeze-safe, runs this week regardless of every other ruling. The six queries (companion file, schema-true, covering-index-aware): Q0 physical column order; Q1 ISRC + Spotify fill by tier with attempted/never-attempted splits; Q2 Apple + MBID + Discogs fill; Q3 platform breadth per row + the ISRC-collision histogram; Q4 entity-grain coverage; **Q5 (panel addition) MBID fill by birth path** (`crawler` vs `spotify-born`) — the query that tests whether an MBID leg serves certified rows at all.

**Go/no-go, pre-stated:** if fewer than **2,000 rows carry two or more verified platform links**, no new public artifact is built — the archive publishes nothing (option 1) or at most the `list_tracks` extension (option 2), and this RFC's § Alternatives records why. If the threshold passes, the operator chooses from the ladder with the numbers in hand. (The certified-only corpus is ~84 findings — a certified-only surface fails this threshold by construction, which is why the Unlit confirmation in § Decisions matters.)

## 3. Unit 1a — the freeze-safe trues-ups (ship now, artifact or no artifact)

True regardless of what gets built; both close attempt-state asymmetries the sweeps themselves care about. All columns nullable, no `.default()` (the ~125-index rebuild trap).

1. **`tracks.isrc_attempted_at` — with its semantics and backfill named** (the panel's day-one blocker). An "attempt" is: any ISRC fill path concluding (publish's Spotify/Deezer lookups; the crawl's MB read; the Deezer-recovery rung). Rows outside every fill path's reach (the worklist's permanent exclusions) are `refused`, not `unattempted` — see the shared-predicate rule in Unit 2. Legacy rows: a one-time backfill stamps rows whose ISRC is non-null (the fill demonstrably ran); ISRC-less legacy rows honestly read `unattempted` and drain as the sweeps revisit them.
2. **Discogs catalogue-row bookkeeping** — mirror the Apple four-column reliability set onto `tracks` (the fix that table already got once). Without it, catalogue rows cannot distinguish `absent` from `unattempted` for Discogs.

**Unit 1b — the contract-serving columns, built only with a ruled-in artifact:** the persisted provenance pair using the **five-member** `AnchorReviewSource` enum ("apify" included — the Apify rung never passes through `resolveAnchorFree`) plus `verifiedBy` extended with `"search-subset"` (the ±1s proper-subset gate is a different confidence than the full gate) and `"operator"` (written by `resolve_anchor_review`, today the best-provenance anchors in the corpus and the draft would have mislabeled them `unknown-legacy`); `spotify_anchored_at` (the anchor-hit time — `spotify_anchor_attempted_at` is a _last-attempt_ stamp and is NULL on every publish-born finding); and the Deezer pair (`deezer_track_id` + its own attempt bookkeeping — without which Deezer ships `unsupported`, because its write path's four failure modes are indistinguishable today).

## 4. Unit 2 — the honest-identity contract (the spec, whichever artifact carries it)

**Naming (corrected):** `get_track_identity` — `resolve` is a registered _write_ verb ("walks external authorities… writing") and a pure read named `resolve_track` inverts a convention whose value is one meaning per verb; the linter cannot catch it because it checks the token, not the meaning. Companion: `get_identity_coverage`. Run the naming-convention-linter before the URL exists — the URL is the one thing that cannot change after the first integrator.

**Keys:** `?isrc=` | `?mbid=` | `?logId=`, tolerant input schema with exclusivity + format checks and the `422` thrown **in-handler** (oRPC schema rejection emits 400; the `search_tracks` contract comment is the precedent). **The honest primary key is Fluncle's own `track_id`** — ISRC and MBID are co-equal non-unique secondary indexes that both return arrays (MBID is not unique either: a crawler row and a Spotify-born finding can carry the same MBID, and the dedupe sweep folds on ISRC, not MBID).

**`recordings[]` — always an array, with `relation` per entry:** `"canonical" | "duplicate-of:<trackId>" | "ambiguous"`, canonical first. `duplicate_of_track_id` is a verdict the archive already holds (catalogue row ≡ finding by exact ISRC) — serving it as "ambiguous" would discard a ruling; genuinely distinct recordings sharing an ISRC (~8% measured) are the true `ambiguous`. Carry `distinctRecordings` beside `recordings.length`. Each entry: `certified` (the strong findings-with-Log-ID sense) **and** `logId` (nullable — the straggler window is real; never infer one from the other).

**Per-platform link states — four, plus `refused`:**

| State         | Means                                                | Computability rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `verified`    | held, with `verification: { method, at, atMeaning }` | `method` from the persisted rung (incl. `search-subset`, `operator`, `pk-derived` for crawler-born MBIDs, `unknown-legacy`); `at` nullable; **`atMeaning: "verified" \| "attempted" \| null`** so an attempt stamp is never read as a verification time                                                                                                   |
| `absent`      | looked, nothing cleared the bar                      | `{ attempts, lastAttemptedAt, retry: "capped"\|"recheckable"\|"single-shot", cap, terminal }` — **`terminal` is `null` wherever no column can back it**; Apple is `recheckable` (never terminal, by its own schema doctrine); MBID is `single-shot`; the Spotify `attempts` served must be a monotone count, never the requeue-decremented budget counter |
| `refused`     | permanently never offered to the sweeps              | derived from **the same exported SQL fragment `kindClause("anchor")` uses** (all five exclusions: cap, placeholder credit, dismissed, duplicate, no-duration — the `REC_ELIGIBLE_WHERE` precedent), with a test that the contract's `refused` and the worklist's exclusion agree row-for-row; `reason` is a closed machine enum, no tier nouns            |
| `unattempted` | never looked, might yet                              | the honest default only where a fill path could still reach the row                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `unsupported` | no rung exists                                       | Tidal; Deezer until 1b's bookkeeping ships; Apple until the ADPLA clause is read                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |

**Cut from the draft, per panel:** `disputed` (it published the operator's _unruled review inbox_ — "EVIDENCE, NEVER AN ANCHOR… only the operator binds it" — structurally unlit-only, hence Fluncle-authored suspicion about tracks he never certified; a ruled-accepted review becomes `verified` with `method: "operator"`, and that is the only public path for it) and `ended` (no liveness machinery exists anywhere; nothing may be promised that nothing computes).

**The Spotify key rides a hop:** links serve as `https://www.fluncle.com/out/spotify/<trackId>` — a 302 through Fluncle. The mapping stays behind a redirect, the referral is attributed, abuse is measurable, and the response frames itself as _evidence of what Fluncle observed_, never a directory. A `meta.attribution` field discharges nothing (attribution is a display obligation binding whoever renders); the consumer-side duty lives in `/terms` (Unit 4).

**`get_identity_coverage`:** the denominator is **the resolvable set** — `dismissed_at is null and duplicate_of_track_id is null`, split by tier, with a per-platform _attempted_ sub-denominator so `verified/attempted` and `verified/resolvable` are both visible, and a test that every denominator row resolves non-404. **Served from a daily snapshot** (the `catalogue_snapshots` precedent), never a live scan — the live aggregate is an uncovered walk of `tracks` dragging the post-blob columns, i.e. the measured 12–19s shape, on a public anonymous op.

## 5. Unit 3 — the dials, the detector, and the cache ruling

- Two dials on the existing atomic limiter, IP-bucketed (`hash(cf-connecting-ip)`): a burst limit and a daily ceiling. Named honestly: the in-repo precedents are `search_archive` (IP-keyed, single-dial) and `/api/chat` (two-dial, _user_-keyed) — **a two-dial IP surface is new**, and shared-NAT/CI-egress callers sharing one daily budget plus the `"unknown"`-bucket fallback are support realities to document.
- **The write cost is 2 Turso writes per read** on a path that carries none today — the hosted measurement sizes that pair (the shape itself already runs in prod on `search_archive`; this is sizing for machine-caller RPS, not proof of concept). Deliverable in the same unit: **a `rate_limit_counters` prune** — nothing deletes from that table today, and two rows per unique IP per day forever is the growing table the draft never named.
- **The abuse detector ships with the dials, tested** (the tripwire law: a detector is unproven until a synthetic failure fires it): a named threshold on the resolver's rate-limit events wired to an alert that reaches the operator, with a test that fires it. The key layer (§6) stays decoupled _only because_ this detector exists; an untested trigger would be the blind-detector failure shape.
- **Cache ruling (decided here, not delegated):** launch **uncached**. The "extend `edge-cache.ts`" option was mis-scoped in the draft — `handleOrpc` returns before the edge-cache block ever runs, so caching any oRPC op means opening the dispatch spine in `server.ts`, a change touching every contract op. Not for this build; revisit only if measured load demands it.

## 6. Unit 3b — the free key layer (decoupled, with an honest trigger)

Off the critical path **unless the Spotify key ships raw** (it does not — the `/out/` hop is Unit 2's design; if that hop were ever dropped, keys move into the launch gate). Triggers, named: the tested abuse alert firing; the first integrator asking for headroom; or wanting the unblock it hands the two other parked items (authenticated MCP's "dedicated auth contract", public `/chat`'s per-caller ceiling). Mechanics when it fires: an `api_keys` table (SHA-256 hash PK, issue/revoke, contact email), `bucket = keyId`, and the auth-tier addition — which is **a new middleware + `STATIC_MIDDLEWARE_TIERS` + `EXPECTED_TIERS` entries** (the tier is derived from the running router, not declared), not one enum line. Zero new bindings, no Stripe, no KV, no DO.

## 7. Unit 4 — terms, copy, and the fan-out (the gates, enumerated)

**`/terms` is a launch blocker under every posture — three edits, not one** (the draft quoted the right page and the wrong sentence): the fair-use number on the "reasonable use" line; a carve-out on the _user-side licence grant_ ("all for your own personal, non-commercial use" — which as written forbids every realistic integrator) stating what an API consumer may do commercially with the API while the archive's _content_ stays non-commercial; and a consumer-side attribution + platform link-back duty. The self-description sentence ("a personal, non-commercial archive") genuinely stands under posture A — it was never the operative clause.

**Every public string in this build is copy-gated** (the skill before drafting, canon-reviewer blocking on the diff): the `/docs` section, both ops' OpenAPI summaries/descriptions (they render live in `/docs/api`), the 404 and 422 bodies, the `reason`/`method` enums (closed machine tokens, no tier nouns), the llms.txt line, the registry entry description, the `/terms` edits. **The Engine-Room Rule constraint, explicit:** `resolve` is engine-room vocabulary — legal as an operationId, path, or CLI verb; forbidden in any `/docs` heading, card title, error message, or `/terms` sentence. **The 404 carries no submission affordance** unless resolver-sourced submissions are partitioned from crew submissions — machine callers must not pollute the operator's triage queue.

Registry + surfaces fan-out via the fluncle-surfaces checklist; `PUBLIC_UNAUTH_OPS` and `PUBLIC_OPERATION_IDS` both named in the acceptance criteria (the latter fails only the deploy build — the known trap).

## § Alternatives — the ladder the artifact decision chooses from

| Option                                                                                                                             | Cost        | New surface       | What it captures                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1. Nothing (after Unit 0 + 1a)                                                                                                     | 0           | no                | the trues-ups keep the ledger honest for Fluncle's own use                                                         |
| 2. **Extend the existing ops** — identity fields on the catalogue row schema + `?isrc=`/`?mbid=` keys on `get_track`/`list_tracks` | hours       | no                | keyed lookup + bulk enumeration; zero new gates/docs/terms scope beyond the carve-out                              |
| 3. **A published identity dump** — nightly `identity.jsonl` on R2, linked from `/docs` + llms.txt                                  | ~a day      | a file            | everything an integrator at this corpus size wants (they would cache anyway); no rate-limit surface, no abuse case |
| 4. **A `/resolve` reader page** — the honest-state envelope rendered for humans, crawlable                                         | ~a day      | a reader page     | citations + AEO; a _reader_ surface, which is what the overhaul is about                                           |
| 5. **The new keyed endpoint** (Units 2+3 in full)                                                                                  | the largest | yes, full fan-out | everything above **plus** the five-state envelope as a machine contract                                            |

The honest-negative envelope is the only thing option 5 adds over 2+3+4 combined — and options 3+4 are the two that feed the September label-outreach story directly ("here is your back catalogue's verified identity map, free, correct — a page per release and the dump behind it"). That synergy is a genuine argument for 3+4 _inside_ the go-to-market beat rather than 5 ahead of it. The RFC does not pre-decide; Unit 0's numbers and the operator's September priorities do.

## Sequencing & ownership

1. **This week, no rulings needed:** commit the evidence file; run Unit 0; ship Unit 1a. All read-only or write-path truth, all freeze-safe without the label doing work.
2. **Then the operator rules** (§ Decisions) with numbers in hand.
3. **If an artifact is ruled in:** Unit 1b → Unit 2 + Unit 3 together (the envelope without dials hands the asset away unbounded; the dials without the envelope bound nothing) → Unit 4 on the same merge. The hosted-proof set is a **triple**: the MBID value index build, the 2-writes-per-read dial load, and the coverage snapshot's plan.
4. **Unit 3b on its tested trigger**, strictly before any money, which remains a separate posture-B/C conversation this RFC recommends against opening now.

## Decisions needed BEFORE handoff

1. **Confirm (not decide) the Unlit reading.** Settled by six shipped public ops (`list_tracks` + its documented `?certified=false` filter, `list_fresh`, `list_mixable_tracks`, `list_set_tracks`, `search_archive`, `search_tracks`) all serving title/artists/links for uncertified rows with the doctrine written into the contracts. The binding constraint travels: the `certified` boolean is the only tier carrier. A "no" here would require withdrawing shipped surfaces and is out of scope.
2. **The artifact** (§ Alternatives 1–5), ruled after Unit 0, weighed against the September beats — options 3+4 feed label outreach; option 5 is a developer surface the overhaul's ladder does not name.
3. **The posture: A recommended, now.** B (money buys bulk/convenience, never access — the MusicBrainz shape) is the only credible later step; C is evidenced against (Linktree's verdict, indie WTP ≈ 0, the structural Spotify clause, the fixed tax overhead, the canon's missing commercial register, every comparable ending acquired/hibernated/net-negative).
4. **The freeze:** build the ruled-in artifact now (Units 0/1a are freeze-safe regardless; the sunset vacuum is permanent either way) or after the overhaul. A calm call — nothing races.
5. **The retirement trigger** (panel addition, and the cheapest insurance in the doc): if fewer than N distinct callers/consumers over 90 days post-launch, the surface and its fan-out are deleted; the Unit 1 columns stay, because they are truthful regardless. Pick N.
6. **Correctness ownership:** the wrong-answer channel (a bad `verified` link reported by an integrator) — the operator's inbox, a `/docs` contact line, or the keys layer's contact emails. Name it or the contract's verification promise is unowned.

## Acceptance criteria

- Unit 0's six queries run against hosted prod; numbers + `EXPLAIN` outputs land in the companion file; the go/no-go threshold is applied as pre-stated.
- Unit 1a ships with the attempt-semantics documented on the columns, the legacy backfill executed, and integration tests through real migrations.
- If ruled in: the contract's states are exhaustively fixtured (verified per method incl. operator and search-subset; absent per retry class with `terminal: null` where uncomputable; refused agreeing row-for-row with the shared worklist predicate; unattempted; unsupported; ambiguous-vs-duplicate relations; the straggler window), the `/out/` hop serves and logs, both dials + the prune + the tested abuse alert land together, the five gates pass (`PUBLIC_UNAUTH_OPS` and `PUBLIC_OPERATION_IDS` included), the hosted triple is recorded, all copy gates run with canon-reviewer blocking, and the `/terms` three-edit set ships with the artifact — never after it.
- This RFC is deleted on ship (the prune rule); the companion evidence file stays.

## Risks — rewritten per panel

- **The Spotify exposure is a change in kind, not degree, and the standing bulk exposure is not this build.** `list_tracks` already publicly enumerates every Spotify URL Fluncle holds; what a keyed identity surface adds is Fluncle acting as a _cross-identifier mapping authority_ — the shape platform metadata terms target, free or not. Mitigations that are real: the `/out/` hop, the terms duty, the evidence framing, option-2/3/4 artifacts being less resolver-shaped than option 5. Mitigation the draft claimed and the panel struck: there is **no "legal firewall"** in the provenance column — every rung touches Spotify somewhere; the column is provenance, and that is enough reason to keep it.
- **Apple is blocked, honestly:** no Apple links to third parties until ADPLA §3.3.6(D) is read verbatim (`unsupported` until then). Tidal likewise pending its terms.
- **Deezer's terms bar any commercial benefit** — an exposure that already exists via the recovery rung, independent of this RFC; flagged for the operator regardless of posture.
- **Demand may be ~zero.** The build's value calibration survives that (small build, durable trues-ups, the reach asset, the unblocked substrate) — but the retirement trigger exists because "probably wrong cheaply" is the honest posture.
