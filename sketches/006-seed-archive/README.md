## Variant: Seed & Archive

### Design stance
The seed hero, compacted, with the archive feed restored as the main content: the seed results live in a "Close in sound" side panel instead of owning the page. Discovery and the logbook share the front door.

### Key choices
- Layout: compact seed hero → two-column body (latest findings feed | close-in-sound panel + listen card)
- Typography: Oxanium headline, Space Grotesk rows, Monaspace Krypton for the proximity readouts and presets
- Color: canon tokens; gold on the seed CTA, the side-panel kicker, and the proximity values
- Interaction: seed input updates the side panel's sector name live; preset chips; feed rows keep the canon hover behavior

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: the seed gets the hero moment AND the archive stays the page; closest to the canon's "music first" while adding the interactive hook
- Weak at: the seed results are demoted to a side panel, so the "wow" of the full results grid is smaller; the proximity numbers are placeholder data

### Best for
- If you want the interactive seed without giving up the logbook front door.
