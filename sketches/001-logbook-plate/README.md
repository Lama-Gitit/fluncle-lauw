## Variant: Logbook Plate

### Design stance
The ratified canon evolved: the home page stays one recovered logbook plate, with the new sitemap nav moved into a top bar. The cover stays the hero, the feed is the page.

### Key choices
- Layout: two-column plate (cover + link hub | feed field), centered, max-w-7xl
- Typography: Oxanium nameplate + FOUND stamp, Space Grotesk reading, tabular Log IDs
- Color: full DESIGN.md token set, gold at ~1.3% of screen (One Sun budget)
- Interaction: cover story badge, row hovers (Gold Veil, art scale 1.06, caret drift), load-more row

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: continuity with the shipped product, zero canon risk, the archive IS the page
- Weak at: the sitemap nav (Launch Control, Galaxy, Listen, Browse, Logbook, Builder) is present but the page does not lean into the "launch" metaphor

### Best for
- The safe evolution: keep everything that works, add the new nav. Lowest risk of the four.
