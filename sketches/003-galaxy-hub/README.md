## Variant: Galaxy Hub

### Design stance
The most lore-forward of the four: the cover art is the arrival, the logbook voice speaks in first person, and the archive is framed as sectors of the Galaxy. The feed is still the page, but the fiction leads.

### Key choices
- Layout: arrival (big cover | nameplate + lede) → sector strip (4 logbook chapters) → feed panel
- Typography: Oxanium for the big nameplate, Space Grotesk lede with dotted graph links
- Color: canon tokens; gold on the kicker, the sector numbers, and the primary CTA
- Interaction: cover story badge, sector tiles with a "now" marker on the current sector, row hovers

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: the Nostalgic Cosmos mood; the crew register; the sectors make the logbook chapters tangible
- Weak at: the lede is prose on the front door (the canon keeps home copy minimal); sector names are invented fiction that must match LORE.md; more scroll before the feed

### Best for
- If the goal is "the web's most user-friendly DnB discovery platform" with the story as the hook, this is the emotional version.
