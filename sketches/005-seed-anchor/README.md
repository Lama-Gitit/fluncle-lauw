## Variant: Seed Anchor

### Design stance
The faithful canon recreation of the inspiration mockup you picked: a full-width hero with the interactive seed search front and center, and the sector results as a card grid below. The seed is the page.

### Key choices
- Layout: full-width hero (pill, gradient headline, seed search, preset chips) → sector results header → 3-column track card grid
- Typography: Oxanium for the big headline and sector title, Space Grotesk for reading, Monaspace Krypton for the mono labels (LOG IDs, BPM, presets)
- Color: canon tokens; the inspiration's amber/zinc retinted to Eclipse Gold and warm blacks; gold at ~1.8% of screen
- Interaction: seed input updates the sector name (live), preset chips, Sonar scan button flashes a scanning state, cards lift on hover

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: the exact concept you liked, in Fluncle's own skin; the seed is the hero, zero competition
- Weak at: the archive feed is gone from the front page (the canon's "the tracklist is the page" is set aside); the results grid needs real vector data behind it to ship

### Best for
- The direction you pointed at: wide, interactive, modern, still Fluncle.
